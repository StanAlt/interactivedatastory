## Two Camps, Both Confident

Northwind cut 30,000 corporate roles in October 2026 — about 9% of its corporate staff of 350,000. Honestly, the truth is that the company quietly pointed at AI. It's not a cost story. It's a strategic pivot. Costs rose. Hiring fell. Pay froze. Analysts disagreed on the cause, and one firm said the cuts actually reflected over-hiring in 2021, which actually matters for how you read the 9%.

Contoso offered voluntary exits to about 60,000 employees, roughly 27% of its workforce, according to a trade-press report dated October 26. Make no mistake: the reality is that this is a $4.2 billion restructuring.
